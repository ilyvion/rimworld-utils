# Waylith notices

The source files in this folder are decompiled from RimWorld 1.7's `Waylith.dll` (namespace
`Waylith.Rendering`) and modified: they are moved into the `AssetPackBaker.Waylith` namespace and
made internal. RimWorld credits its texture encoders (`Bc1BlockEncoder`, `Bc4BlockEncoder`,
`Bc7BlockEncoder`, `Bc7PackFlags`) as derived from Basis Universal and bc7enc_rdo, under the
notices below.
